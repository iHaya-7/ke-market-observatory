# Stage 2 — Controlled vocabulary

21 October 2026 · Working vocabulary selected for this proposal; team approval pending. Stage 1 remains proposed for approval. These terms are not ontology classes, a taxonomy, or implemented fields.

Method: start with existing system language, distinguish meanings, select preferred terms, and bound them by stakeholder questions, following the local [Ontology Pipeline](../../../Ontology-Pipeline/Ontology-Pipeline.md), “Define purpose and scope” and “The Word hunt: Concept discovery and reconciliation” (lines 740–773). Sources are [Scope](../01-scope/SCOPE.md), [CQs](../01-scope/COMPETENCY_QUESTIONS.md), the [audit](../00-baseline/AS_IS_AUDIT.md) §§3–6, the [protocol](../00-baseline/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md) §1, production JSON, configuration, and code. No evaluation case or private claim material was inspected.

CQ numbers below refer to CQ-01 through CQ-06. “None” in alternatives means no justified synonym is adopted, not that synonyms cannot exist.

## Controlled distinctions and approval

Base salary and base pay are interchangeable labels for the same measure here; total compensation, bonus, equity, and OTE are not synonyms for base pay. Retain the raw source's meaning rather than rewriting it to match a parser bucket. Eligibility concerns an observation; comparability concerns a designated pair; global benchmark inclusion additionally depends on publication/role/geography rules. These are assessment subjects, not additional ontology classes proposed here.

Missing, explicit negative, ambiguous, and unreviewed are distinct states. `false`, `null`, an absent property, and an empty string cannot be freely substituted. The [metadata dictionary](METADATA_DICTIONARY.md) preserves actual legacy behavior rather than imposing new values.

Haya and Lubna should approve these 16 working concepts, their limited aliases, and the separation of attribution, evidence, and assessments. No mapping from machine category names to human labels, new domain hierarchy, or evidence judgment is approved by this document.
