# Stage 0 — Step 0.3A: Evaluation Protocol

Status: proposed design for human approval, 21 October 2026. This document defines evaluation decisions, not an ontology or taxonomy. Scope is limited to job relevance, salary eligibility/comparability, evidence traceability, and review-case detection. No corpus capture, labels, implementation, or scores are produced here.

## Approved reference and measurement boundary

Read alongside [AS_IS_AUDIT.md](AS_IS_AUDIT.md), the frozen [MANIFEST.md](snapshots/20261021T110236218Z-ec7c36e4/MANIFEST.md), [manifest.json](snapshots/20261021T110236218Z-ec7c36e4/manifest.json), and [BASELINE_RESULTS.md](snapshots/20261021T110236218Z-ec7c36e4/BASELINE_RESULTS.md).

Baseline snapshot: `20261021T110236218Z-ec7c36e4`; implementation HEAD: `ee5bd15ff52b6a88e15a2dafab6886422980f557`. Its 21 curated, 20 retained automatic, and 26 published records are overlapping populations, not a ready-made evaluation corpus. Only one published record passes the existing frontend benchmark predicate. Source counts also differ from retained counts; neither supplies an evaluation denominator.

Excluded postings and full historical descriptions are not retained. Historical Recall cannot be reconstructed from these files alone. A new corpus evaluated with the frozen baseline implementation measures prospective performance; it must not be presented as historical accuracy of the snapshot. Current machine approvals, curated flags, and unit-test fixtures are not independent Ground Truth.

## 1. Human-review criteria

Judge the preserved original description and compensation evidence, including responsibilities and context. Do not decide by title alone, keyword counts, company reputation, or existing flags. Record a passage locator and rationale. `Insufficient Evidence` means a required fact cannot be determined reliably; a blank means unreviewed and is never a negative label.

| Decision | Allowed labels and criteria |
| --- | --- |
| `human_relevance` | **Relevant:** substantive responsibilities create, curate, validate, govern, integrate, or use explicit knowledge representations or semantic meaning as a necessary part of the work. **Not relevant:** a complete description shows ordinary software/data/AI work, administrative knowledge management, or commercial/recruiting work with only incidental terminology or no material KE responsibilities. **Insufficient Evidence:** missing/incomplete text, contradictory scope, or vague duties prevent a reliable determination. A complete, clearly unrelated description is a negative, not insufficient evidence. |
| `human_role_scope` | **Core KE:** primary responsibilities concern explicit knowledge/semantic models, ontologies, controlled vocabularies, semantic constraints, or knowledge-graph meaning and structure. **Adjacent technical:** primary work is AI, applications, data/platform engineering, architecture, or governance, with substantive implementation/use of those representations. Both count as Relevant. Generic catalogs, graphs, metadata, or retrieval alone do not establish this connection. **Neither:** Not relevant. **Insufficient Evidence:** relevance or the core/adjacent boundary is unresolved. Relevant can remain definite while role scope is unresolved. No particular vendor or RDF/OWL terminology is required. |
| `human_salary_eligibility` | **Eligible:** an attributable original employer posting or its public recruiting-agent posting explicitly supports a numeric base-pay amount/range, currency, Annual/Monthly period, and applicable pay market/band. Amounts must be finite/nonnegative and ordered; a missing upper bound permits a floor observation. Use USD/GBP/EUR/SAR, matching the frozen benchmark's supported units. **Ineligible:** explicit total compensation/OTE, bonus/equity alone, publisher estimate, unsupported repost, hourly pay without a supported normalization policy, malformed amounts, or a complete posting disclosing no pay. **Insufficient Evidence:** unclear base-versus-total wording, missing source evidence, unresolved units, employer/client attribution, or band/geography. Multiple bands may be assessable only when the preselected band's applicability is explicit; never pool them. |
| `human_comparability` | Judge only the fixed pair named by `comparison_case_id`. **Comparable:** both observations are eligible and support the same gross base-pay measure, same defined country/pay market, materially similar role scope, and same explicitly evidenced career level. Annual/Monthly and supported currencies may be converted with frozen FX. **Not comparable:** an established mismatch or a confirmed ineligible observation. **Insufficient Evidence:** required market, scope, level, attribution, or pay basis is unknown; missing experience is not Junior. **Not applicable:** no pair was assigned. Compare floors by default; compare full ranges only if both ceilings exist and that endpoint was declared before labeling. Conversion alone does not establish comparability. |
| `human_evidence_support` | Judge the real, fixed `claim_text` against its captured evidence. **Supported:** the preserved, attributable source directly supports the material assertion, including the stated responsibility or amount/basis/unit/geography. **Unsupported:** a sufficiently complete source contradicts it or does not substantiate the claim. **Insufficient Evidence:** the source is unavailable/incomplete or the passage/context cannot resolve it. URLs, source dates, titles, and successful API status alone do not prove support. |
| `human_review_required` | **Yes:** unresolved relevance, pay basis/band, comparability, conflicting duplicate evidence, missing source support, or contradiction materially affects a scoped decision and requires human interpretation or additional evidence. A task-level Insufficient Evidence judgment normally requires Yes. **No:** the evidence permits a clear acceptance or rejection without unresolved material issues; clear negatives/ineligible pay need not enter review. **Insufficient Evidence:** even the case identity/triage need cannot be determined. Explain the missing fact. |

Salary eligibility concerns the observation independently of role relevance. A non-relevant job can disclose eligible base pay; a relevant job can have ineligible pay. Saudi observations can have eligible pay evidence but remain outside the frozen global frontend benchmark. Keep these facts separate from final benchmark admission. Historical/open status is not a salary-eligibility requirement in this protocol. Vacancy-status verification is not an additional evaluation dimension; consider it only when an actual scoped claim depends on it.

For uncertain cases, identify the affected fields in `uncertainty_fields` and explain ambiguity in `decision_rationale`. Do not force a binary answer to increase the assessable sample. A definite ineligibility reason can resolve the salary label even when an unrelated field is missing.

## 2. Fixed corpus: proposed size and capture

Target **40 unique real postings**, adjustable to **30–50** subject to availability. Target sampling strata are 15 baseline-accepted, 10 baseline-pending, and 15 baseline-excluded postings. These are sampling strata, never human labels. Select reproducibly within strata using a recorded seed and candidate inventory; record shortages and actual allocations rather than fabricating or silently replacing cases.

Across these overlapping strata, seek clear semantic-role positives, clear unrelated negatives (including technical and nontechnical keyword mentions), adjacent roles, and ambiguous descriptions. Include clear employer base pay, explicit total/OTE or secondary estimates/reposts where encountered in real postings, undisclosed pay, unclear basis/units, and multiple/geographic bands. Seek at least five clearly documented base-pay candidates and five clearly disqualifying pay cases before labeling; these are availability targets, not promises about Ground Truth. Use compatible configured ATS sources initially. If sources cannot supply the mix, obtain approval for adjusted coverage or a separately documented source extension applied identically to both runs.

Capture later, after approval:

1. Recover historical inputs only if authentic original artifacts with credible dates/provenance exist. A page obtained now is a prospective capture, not evidence of its historical contents. Never reconstruct missing text from normalized summaries or keywords.
2. Otherwise use a bounded prospective capture window and source list. Preserve **all raw postings before filtering**, including exclusions, then run the frozen baseline offline to assign sampling strata. No production refresh is needed for evaluation. Preserve a candidate inventory so selection and exclusions remain auditable.
3. For each selected posting preserve the raw ATS response/original HTML or document, complete original title/description, all salary bands and wording, source/job IDs, original URL, UTC capture time, content hash, and precise locators. Retain full evidence for unclear/no-pay cases too. Designate at most one salary observation per posting using a documented location/band rule before human labels or After outputs; retain all other bands as context. If no unique band applies, label the uncertainty rather than choosing the favorable range.
4. Capture any genuine material assertion selected for evidence review as `claim_text`, with its original attribution and candidate evidence locator. Use one fixed claim per posting, covering role scope or compensation across the corpus. Do not invent unsupported claims to fill a stratum. Freeze the claims and required trace fields before evaluating either version.
5. Group duplicate/reposted requisitions and closely related variants before splitting. Count a primary posting once; preserve related evidence under the same group. Unresolved identity remains explicit and must not inflate independent-case counts. Assign 10 development and 30 held-out postings for a 40-case corpus; scale to roughly 25%/75% otherwise. Keep each group wholly in one split and distribute source/outcome strata where feasible.
6. Assign approximately 6–10 fixed salary comparison pairs within the corpus, as availability permits. Store each unordered pair once on its designated case row, reference the other case's ID, and freeze market/scope/level and endpoint criteria before labels. Do not select pairs based on favorable model results. If no meaningful pairs exist, omit quantitative comparability claims.

Freeze a separate corpus manifest with IDs, artifact hashes, selection ledger, splits, pair specifications, claim definitions, and all eligible evaluation denominators. No such corpus or manifest is created in Step 0.3A.

## 3. Empty labeling template and review process

[evaluation-template.csv](evaluation-template.csv) contains only its header. One future row represents one posting. Pair judgments live only on the designated pair row. References resolve to immutable, hashed corpus artifacts plus a page/section/JSON-pointer/text-span locator. Empty salary references mean no artifact supplied, not automatically no disclosed salary.

| CSV fields | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `case_id`, `duplicate_group_id`, `dataset_split` | Permanent case key; related-job group; `development` or `held_out`. Do not encode a predicted label in the ID. |
| `source_id`, `source_job_id`, `source_url`, `captured_at_utc` | Original source and posting identity and UTC capture. Document a stable surrogate if an upstream ID is absent; do not invent an original ID. |
| `original_job_title`, `description_ref`, `salary_evidence_ref` | Verbatim source title and references to full description and original compensation evidence, including no-pay/unclear context. |
| `comparison_case_id`, `claim_text`, `claim_evidence_ref` | Fixed pair partner, one real material claim, and candidate evidence reference. A reference does not predetermine support. |
| `human_relevance`, `human_role_scope`, `human_salary_eligibility`, `human_comparability`, `human_evidence_support`, `human_review_required` | Independent human decisions using Section 1 labels. No machine-derived defaults. |
| `human_review_reasons`, `decision_rationale`, `uncertainty_fields` | Semicolon-separated scoped reasons (`Role relevance`, `Pay evidence`, `Comparability`, `Evidence traceability`); field-specific explanations/passages; semicolon-separated ambiguous/insufficient field names. |
| `reviewer_id`, `reviewed_at_utc`, `label_status` | Reviewer and ISO-8601 UTC review time; `unreviewed`, `needs_adjudication`, or `final`. Final may retain task-level Insufficient Evidence. |
| `adjudicator_id`, `adjudicated_at_utc`, `label_version` | Adjudication identity/time and frozen label version. Preserve initial reviews and any revisions separately; never silently replace them. |

Use development cases to calibrate the rubric, then freeze it before held-out labeling. Two reviewers independently label held-out cases without seeing either system's predictions or sampling stratum. Resolve disagreement by documented consensus or a third reviewer; retain both initial judgments, rationale, and adjudication. Unresolved decisions remain Insufficient Evidence. If only one reviewer is available, mark the exercise provisional and do not claim adjudicated evaluation results.

Store predictions in separate, later artifacts keyed by `run_id` and `case_id`: implementation/checksum identity, raw decisions, relevance label, salary flags/normalized fields, actual review-queue membership/categories, internal review flags, evidence links, output status, and timestamps. Never place system predictions in human-label columns. Human support labels concern the fixed claims; per-run citation/claim checks belong with the output assessments and must not rewrite Ground Truth.

## 4. Output definitions and metrics

Future offline evaluation must produce an accounted-for output for every case, including exclusions. Use the frozen implementation, adapters, inputs, state, source settings, and fixed observation clock. An evaluation wrapper may record outputs but must not alter rules, fill evidence gaps, or credit input metadata as system-generated citations. This section specifies the later procedure; no runner is implemented here.

| Output | Fixed Before/After interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic relevance acceptance | Existing `assess_role()` decision `approved` is positive; `pending` and `excluded` are negative for **automatic acceptance**. Preserve pending separately as abstention/review. A non-null `relevance` string alone is not approval. Report Core/Adjacent/Neither human-versus-system cross-tabs as descriptive error analysis; do not assume existing category names equal human scope. |
| Salary eligibility | Evaluate `salary_info()`'s intrinsic `salaryEligible` decision on each captured input, including jobs later excluded for relevance. True is positive; false/withheld is not eligible for automatic use. Preserve ambiguity flags. This isolates pay assessment from role filtering. |
| Final global benchmark | Separately list the actual `baseEligible()` outputs and exclusions, with the frozen publication gate/manual overrides. Do not call its count intrinsic salary accuracy: geography and role inclusion affect admission. |
| Review detection | Primary prediction is **actual queue membership** for that case, not `reviewRequired`. Use the existing queue categories and match IDs through the frozen identity map; do not let a wrapper add missing queue items. Secondary flag-versus-queue counts diagnose the known routing gap. |
| Traceability | Credit only references actually supplied by the system. They must resolve through the same frozen source-identity map to the source URL/ID, capture date, hashed original artifact, and precise claim-relevant locator. The map may resolve a supplied citation; it must not manufacture a missing one. |

For each task let the assessable set be the same held-out cases with final, definite human labels for that task. Exclude its Insufficient Evidence and unreviewed cases from binary accuracy calculations; retain them in corpus counts, uncertainty reporting, and review detection when review need is definite. Do not exclude a case merely because the system abstains or is wrong. Pending/withheld predictions on definite positives can therefore be false negatives. A true execution/mapping failure is not an exclusion decision: report it and withhold paired accuracy until both runs account for the identical cases. No model-specific denominator changes.

| Metric | Numerator / denominator and reporting rule |
| --- | --- |
| Relevance Precision | `TP / (TP + FP)`, where TP is accepted with human Relevant and FP is accepted with human Not relevant. |
| Relevance Recall | `TP / (TP + FN)`, where FN is human Relevant but not automatically accepted, including pending. This is recall within the fixed corpus, not market-wide or historical recall. |
| Relevance F1 | `2TP / (2TP + FP + FN)`. Publish the underlying confusion counts and abstention count. |
| Salary eligibility accuracy | `(TP + TN) / N_salary`, on definite Eligible/Ineligible labels using the intrinsic salary output above. Include clear no-pay negatives and report positive/negative counts so numerous undisclosed cases cannot conceal poor positive performance. Insufficient Evidence is excluded, with count/reason shown. |
| Evidence traceability coverage | Cases with **all required trace fields resolvable** for their fixed claim divided by **all held-out cases**. Missing output, missing source, or broken locator stays in the denominator and fails coverage. This is linkage coverage, not claim correctness. Report human Supported/Unsupported/Insufficient Evidence counts alongside it; inspect each run's actual cited claims against those fixed source facts. Do not score an unavailable claim as unsupported merely because the capture is incomplete. |
| Review-case detection | On definite Yes/No review labels: TP = queued and Yes; FP = queued and No; FN = not queued and Yes; TN = not queued and No. Report precision, recall, and F1 with the same formulas as above. Report missed-review reasons; never infer resolved review outcomes from queue disappearance. |
| Comparability | Report Comparable/Not comparable/Insufficient Evidence counts over the fixed assigned pairs, with assessable pair denominator; omit unpaired Not applicable rows. Inspect whether benchmark/compare outputs mix human-judged incompatible observations. The baseline has no explicit comparability classifier, so do not invent one or claim comparability accuracy from normalization. A future decision interface would require a preregistered amendment before scoring. |

Zero denominators are `Not measurable`, never zero accuracy. If no human positives or negatives are available, omit two-class performance claims and show counts. Proposed minimum reporting gates: relevance requires at least 20 assessable held-out cases and five of each class; salary and review each require at least 10 assessable cases and three of each class; traceability requires at least 10 held-out cases. Below a gate, show raw counts/error review only, not a headline percentage or improvement claim. These are pragmatic pilot gates, not statistical power guarantees. With fewer than five assessable comparison pairs, show pair reviews only. If exclusions/labels/raw inputs cannot support a metric, state **Not measurable from current corpus** and explain why.

Always report total cases, per-task assessable cases, Insufficient Evidence, unreviewed cases, system abstentions, errors, and actual denominators. Compare Before/After on the same cases using paired error changes (corrected, regressed, unchanged) and percentage-point differences where supported. Treat results as exploratory for this deliberately stratified small corpus; do not infer market prevalence or statistically established superiority.

## 5. Fair-comparison controls

- Freeze the same raw corpus, hashes, case/group IDs, splits, claims, comparison pairs, Ground Truth version, criteria, and output definitions for both runs. Keep held-out labels inaccessible to implementers while tuning KE changes. Tune only on development cases; do not repeatedly inspect held-out errors and report the tuned score as an untouched test. After test feedback is used for tuning, a new independent held-out corpus is required for a fresh evaluation claim.
- Recover the Before rules from the snapshot's implementation HEAD and verify its recorded code/configuration hashes and checkout line endings. Keep the approved snapshot and seal unchanged. Record the After implementation and the specific KE changes; unrelated improvements, new sources, or extraction changes would confound attribution and must be evaluated separately or disclosed.
- Replay identical captured inputs offline with fixed adapter settings, initial normalized/curated state, manual override policy, observation time, and source-error behavior. Preserve legacy/missing decisions explicitly. Keep manual overrides frozen; report automatic decisions separately from any final curated/manual inclusion. Do not mix new production records or fresher source pages into one run.
- Hold the published snapshot's SAR rates constant: `SAR=1`, `USD=3.75`, `GBP=5.005005`, `EUR=4.205639` (reference date `2026-10-20`). Use the same Annual/Monthly conversion and rounding for both runs. Currency conversion does not make jobs, pay markets, experience levels, or total/base compensation interchangeable.
- Record every manual correction with case ID, old/new value, reason, supporting artifact, reviewer/date, and version. Preserve earlier labels. A necessary label/corpus correction creates a new approved version and requires rerunning both systems on identical corrected inputs; never silently update one side or edit the historical snapshot.

## 6. Decisions to approve before capture or labeling

Approve the relevance boundary (including substantive adjacent technical work), intrinsic employer-base salary policy, pair comparability requirements, case-level traceability definition, and actual-queue review target. Confirm source list/capture window, the feasible 30–50-case allocation, grouped development/held-out split, reviewers/adjudicator, and pilot reporting gates. Decide how to handle source or salary-positive shortages before interpreting results.

Approval of this design does not assert that sources, raw historical inputs, labels, or assessable sample sizes already exist. Step 0.3A ends with this protocol and an empty CSV template; capture, labeling, evaluation execution, and KE implementation are later work.
