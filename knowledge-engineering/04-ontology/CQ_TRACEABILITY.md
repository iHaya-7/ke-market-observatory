## Shared provenance and uncertainty

“Evidence classes” means EvidencePassage and EvidenceArtifact; “assessment classes” means Assessment with explicit MachineAssessment/HumanAssessment origin when known. Every future determination has target, kind, outcome when reviewed, criteria reference, rationale, evidence and material unresolved facts. Reuse `prov:wasAttributedTo` for responsible agent, `prov:generatedAtTime` for record generation, `prov:wasGeneratedBy` for identified run/review activity, and `owl:versionInfo` for version. Human membership alone establishes neither reviewer independence nor final Ground Truth.

A complete trace chain needs original source identity/URL, publisher/context, preserved artifact location/checksum/capture time and resolvable passage locator. A URL, summary or source-success flag cannot fill missing evidence. Missing values stay unknown; explicit negatives/Insufficient Evidence are judgments; absent outcomes stay unreviewed. Separate independent and later adjudicated assessments preserve disagreements and history.

## Minimality and acceptance boundary

All 14 custom classes are justified above. Each of the 16 object and 29 datatype properties has CQ references and provenance requirements in ONTOLOGY.md and its Turtle comment. The only local subclass commitments are MachineAssessment/HumanAssessment under Assessment; selected record classes also specialize `prov:Entity`. Shared metadata avoids a class per CQ, label, uncertainty reason, compensation component or SKOS concept.

Design coverage means the schema can express the required evidence and judgments when independently established. It does not assert those data exist or that any CQ has been answered correctly. Later approved validation may check provenance completeness, intended pair size and value conventions. Later evaluation must preserve the unchanged protocol and held-out boundary. No such implementation, data population or scoring occurs here.
